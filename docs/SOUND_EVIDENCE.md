## Our engineering stance
- Free numeric recipes in the mission are **PROPOSED** starting points; each is tuned in the firmware and its changes recorded in `docs/SOUND_LOG.md`.
- No claim is made that any recipe reproduces a specific artist's patch or a circuit-accurate hardware emulation.
- French and Italian reference groups are kept as separate listening sets, not as proof of two universal national recipes.
